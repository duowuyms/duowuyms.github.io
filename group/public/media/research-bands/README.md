# Research band figures

Complete figures shown in the three homepage research bands. Each was rendered from the public paper PDF with PyMuPDF and clipped to the figure's own bounds; captions, page text, and surrounding whitespace are excluded, and nothing inside the figure is cropped or edited. Files are 1800px wide WebP.
